6. Zone Reports — PDF and Excel
Zones to export - tick one or more: each zone is its own PDF; several zones download as one ZIP of separate PDFs.
Press "Load zones" (step 3) to list the zones.
PDF and Excel are separate downloads (one request each); the same snapshot code means the same data. "Download PDF" exports the ticked zones as DRAFT (or FINAL). "Approve Report & Export" is your approval for distribution: the exported PDFs say APPROVED with your name, the time and a reference, and the approved snapshot is stored. Approval never changes a status (an UNMATCHED stays UNMATCHED) and is separate from Finalize (step 7). Fingerprints whose Area has no zone are never put in a zone's report: tick "UNASSIGNED / NEEDS CLASSIFICATION" to review them in their own file.
