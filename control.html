Manual Manpower Plan / إضافة خطة قوى عاملة يدويًا
For an existing event whose Manpower plan is missing or incomplete (admin only). The Daily Log values that are valid are shown and never changed; what you enter completes the plan the system uses (expected headcount, Morning / Night, shift hours) and is recorded under your name. It never adds attendance and never counts an employee.
6. Zone Reports — PDF and Excel
Zones to export - tick one or more: each zone is its own PDF; several zones download as one ZIP of separate PDFs.
Press "Load zones" (step 3) to list the zones.
PDF and Excel are separate downloads (one request each); the same snapshot code means the same data. "Download PDF" exports the ticked zones as DRAFT (or FINAL). "Approve Report & Export" is your approval for distribution: your name, the time, a reference and the approved snapshot are stored (Report_Approvals); the management PDF itself shows only the date, the zone and the figures. Approval never changes a status (hours, approvals and differences stay exactly as computed) and is separate from Finalize (step 7). Fingerprints whose Area has no zone are never put in a zone's report: tick "UNASSIGNED / NEEDS CLASSIFICATION" to review them in their own file.
Attendance Supervisors / مشرفو الحضور
Several attendance supervisors can work at the same time (page: supervisor.html), each with all zones or only chosen zones, and each with a personal access link shown once. Adding a supervisor never removes the others. A supervisor edits and approves attendance times from the Attendance records list. With "Direct approval" the supervisor may also correct records the Zone Manager has not approved yet (Pending / Rejected); the Zone Manager decision stays unchanged. An administrator can intervene explicitly. Admin only.

إدارة استثناءات الموظفين — Employee Exclusions
Excluded Employee Codes are left out of every attendance output: zones, zone matching / unassigned, Zone Manager review links and approvals, statistics, reports, PDF and Excel. Employees are matched by Employee Code only. The original fingerprints are never changed, and FINAL / approved snapshots stay as issued. Admin only.
